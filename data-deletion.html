OnePost - Data Deletion

This page explains how users can disconnect their social media accounts from OnePost and request deletion of data associated with their use of the application.

1. Disconnecting a Social Media Account

Users can revoke OnePost's access to a connected social media account through the account or application settings provided by the respective social media platform.

For platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube, users can review their connected applications and revoke OnePost's authorization using the controls provided by that platform.

2. Locally Stored Data

OnePost is a desktop application and may store information required for application functionality locally on the user's computer.

Users who no longer wish to use OnePost may remove locally stored OnePost application data from their computer.

3. Requesting Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of data associated with your use of OnePost, please send an email to:

dubert@freenet.de

Use the subject line "OnePost Data Deletion Request".

Please provide enough information to identify the account or connection associated with the request. Do not send passwords or social media account credentials.

4. Processing the Request

Data deletion requests will be reviewed and processed as appropriate. Where relevant data is controlled directly by a third-party social media platform, users may also need to use the deletion or account controls provided by that platform.

5. Questions

Questions concerning data deletion or privacy can also be sent to dubert@freenet.de.
