One Post - Data Deletion

Last updated: August 31, 2026

One Post is a desktop application that allows users to connect supported social media accounts and publish their own video content.

Users remain in control of their connected accounts and locally stored application data.

Disconnecting a Social Media Account

Users can disconnect or revoke One Post access to a connected social media account at any time.

Access may be revoked through the account or security settings provided by the respective social media platform.

After authorization has been revoked, One Post can no longer use that authorization to access the connected account.

Locally Stored Data

One Post may store application settings, authorization information and other data required for application functionality on the user's computer.

Users who no longer wish to use One Post may remove locally stored application data from their computer.

Removing local application data may also remove saved account connections and settings from One Post.

Temporary Video Files

For upload methods that require a publicly accessible video URL, One Post may temporarily make a user-selected video available through an external hosting service so that the selected social media platform can retrieve the file.

These temporary files are not intended to be stored permanently and may be removed after they are no longer required for the requested upload or publishing process.

Requesting Data Deletion

If you would like assistance with deleting information associated with One Post, you can submit a data deletion request by email.

Send the request to:

dubert@freenet.de

Please use the subject: One Post Data Deletion Request

Include enough information to identify the request, but do not send passwords or other sensitive login credentials.

Processing the Request

Data deletion requests will be reviewed and processed as appropriate based on the information controlled by One Post.

One Post cannot directly delete information that is controlled exclusively by a third-party social media platform. Users may need to use the account or privacy controls provided by that platform for such information.

Questions

Questions regarding data deletion or One Post can be sent to:

dubert@freenet.de
