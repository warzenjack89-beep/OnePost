Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OnePost handles information when users connect their social media accounts and use the OnePost desktop application.

1. About OnePost

OnePost is a desktop application that allows users to prepare, upload, and publish their own video content to supported social media platforms.

Users choose which accounts to connect, which video files to use, and which publishing actions to perform.

2. Social Media Account Data

When a user connects a supported social media account, OnePost may receive information necessary to authenticate the user and provide the requested features.

For TikTok, this may include basic account information and authorization tokens required to access TikTok APIs on behalf of the authorized user.

OnePost only requests access required for the features offered by the application.

3. Authorization Tokens

Authorization tokens may be stored locally on the user's device when necessary to maintain a connection to an authorized social media account.

These tokens are used only to perform actions requested by the user through OnePost and are not sold to third parties.

4. Video Content

Users select their own video files from their computer. OnePost processes these files only for the purpose of preparing and uploading the content to platforms selected by the user.

For upload methods that require a publicly accessible video URL, OnePost may temporarily make a selected video available through an external hosting service so that the selected social media platform can retrieve the file.

Temporary files are used only for the requested publishing operation and are not intended to be retained permanently.

5. TikTok Integration

OnePost uses TikTok APIs only after the user has authorized OnePost through TikTok's official authorization process.

OnePost does not publish content to TikTok without an action initiated and confirmed by the user.

6. Third-Party Services

OnePost may communicate with third-party services and social media platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, and other supported services.

Information transmitted to these services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

7. Data Sharing

OnePost does not sell users' personal information.

Information is shared with third-party platforms only when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, such as authentication, video uploading, or publishing.

8. Data Retention

OnePost is designed to minimize the storage of user information. Data required for application functionality may be stored locally on the user's computer.

Temporary video files or temporary online resources used during an upload are not intended for permanent storage and may be removed after they are no longer required for the publishing process.

9. Data Deletion

Users may disconnect their social media accounts and remove locally stored OnePost application data from their computer.

Information about requesting deletion of data associated with OnePost is available on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

10. Security

OnePost uses reasonable technical measures to protect authentication information and user data during normal application use. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

OnePost is not specifically designed for children. Users must comply with the age requirements of the social media platforms they choose to connect.

12. Open Source

OnePost is intended to be an open-source project. Available source code may be reviewed to better understand how the application operates.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as OnePost develops, changes its functionality, or adds support for additional platforms.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or OnePost's handling of data can be submitted through the OnePost project support or repository page.
