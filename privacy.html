Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OnePost handles information when users connect their social media accounts and use the application.

1. About OnePost

OnePost is a desktop application that helps users upload and publish their own video content to supported social media platforms.

2. Social Media Account Data

When a user connects a supported social media account, OnePost may receive information required to authenticate the user and perform functions authorized by the user.

For TikTok, this may include basic account information and authorization tokens required to use TikTok's APIs.

3. Video Content

Users select their own video files for uploading. OnePost processes these files only for the purpose of sending the content to the social media platforms selected by the user.

4. Authorization

OnePost only accesses or publishes to a social media account after the user has authorized the application through the platform's official authorization process.

5. Third-Party Services

OnePost may communicate with third-party platforms such as TikTok and YouTube. Use of those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

OnePost does not sell users' personal information. Information is transmitted to social media platforms only as necessary to perform actions requested by the user.

7. Open Source

OnePost is intended to be an open-source project. Users can review the available source code to understand how the application operates.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as OnePost develops or adds support for additional platforms.
