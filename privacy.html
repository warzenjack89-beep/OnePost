Privacy Policy - One Post

Last updated: August 31, 2026

About One Post

One Post is a desktop application that allows users to prepare and publish their own video content to supported social media platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how One Post handles information when users connect social media accounts and use the application's publishing features.

Social Media Account Data

When a user connects a supported social media account, One Post may receive limited account information made available by that platform's official API.

This information may include basic profile information and identifiers necessary to connect the account and provide the requested functionality.

Authorization Tokens

One Post may store authorization tokens required to communicate with supported social media APIs on behalf of the user.

These tokens are used only for actions authorized by the user and are not intended to be sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Video Content

Users select their own video files from their computer. One Post processes selected files only as necessary to perform the upload or publishing action requested by the user.

For upload methods that require a publicly accessible video URL, One Post may temporarily make a selected video available through an external hosting service so that the relevant social media platform can retrieve the file.

Temporary files used for this purpose are not intended to be retained permanently.

TikTok Integration

One Post uses TikTok's official authorization process and TikTok APIs for supported TikTok functionality.

One Post only accesses TikTok information and performs TikTok actions that have been authorized by the user.

Users remain responsible for selecting their content and initiating any upload or publishing action.

Third-Party Services

One Post interacts with third-party services and social media platforms. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

One Post does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms. Users should review the policies of the services they choose to connect.

Data Sharing

One Post does not sell personal information.

Information is shared with third-party platforms only when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, such as account authorization, video uploading or publishing.

Data Retention

One Post stores only information required for application functionality. Locally stored application data may remain on the user's computer until it is removed by the user.

Authorization data may remain stored until the connected account is disconnected, authorization expires, or the locally stored application data is removed.

Data Deletion

Users may disconnect their connected social media accounts and remove locally stored One Post data.

Detailed instructions and contact information for data deletion requests are available on the Data Deletion page.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by One Post. However, no software or online transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

One Post is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or the requirements of connected social media platforms.

Open Source

One Post is intended to be an open-source desktop application. Available source code may be reviewed to better understand how the application handles data and interacts with supported services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when One Post functionality or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
