OnePost

Publish your videos to multiple social media platforms from one desktop application.

About OnePost

OnePost is a desktop application designed to simplify publishing video content to social media platforms.

Users select their own video files, connect their own social media accounts, and choose where their content should be published.

TikTok Integration

OnePost uses the TikTok Content Posting API to allow authorized users to upload and publish their own video content to their TikTok account.

OnePost does not publish content without the user's authorization.
